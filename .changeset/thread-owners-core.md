New `ThreadOwnerRegistry` interface (`claim`, first claimant of a thread id owns it; `get`) with `createInMemoryThreadOwnerRegistry`. The node policy gains `allowedTools` (agent function tools and builtins mapped onto them, `TOOL_BLOCKED`), `allowedFunctions` (`function` / `code` nodes, `FUNCTION_BLOCKED`) and `blockHostExec` (workshop `git diff`, `HOST_EXEC_BLOCKED`), checked when the graph is built and again when it runs; the service tools refuse to start, stop or restart anything when the policy blocks `service` nodes. `guardedFetch` stops at `maxResponseBytes` (default 10 MiB, `OUTBOUND_RESPONSE_TOO_LARGE`) and `timeoutMs` (default 30 s, `OUTBOUND_TIMEOUT`).

**Breaking:** `run()` and `stream()` refuse input that sets a reserved state channel (`__services`, `__worktrees`, `__agentReplay`, `__once`, `__mapCollect`, `__mapTask`, `__issues`, `__interrupt__`) with `RESERVED_CHANNEL_BLOCKED`, policy or not; these channels are engine bookkeeping and were never meant as input. Under a node policy, graphs may not declare or write those channels (`RESERVED_CHANNEL_BLOCKED`), `runtime.services.rehydrateOnResume` and `runtime.worktrees.cleanupOnEnd` are refused when the policy blocks `service` / `worktree` nodes or host programs (`HOST_EXEC_BLOCKED`), saved service entries are not restarted and saved worktrees are not removed for such a graph, and a `hitl` review `diff` that runs `git diff` is refused under `blockHostExec` (a review with no `diff` skips the workspace diff instead). New helpers: `assertGraphRuntimeAllowed`, `assertNoReservedChannels`, `assertNoReservedInput`, `hostExecAllowed`, `collectNodeWriteChannels`.
